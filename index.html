A subheading in italic Cormorant, quiet and editorial.

.t-subhead · Cormorant Garamond 500 italic · 1.3–1.55rem

Body text is set in Cormorant Garamond at a generous size, because its small x-height needs room. It is meant for stories, captions and the warmer parts of the archive — the places where a person is speaking rather than a department.

.t-body · Cormorant Garamond 500 · 1.25rem (1.3 on desktop) · lh 1.55

Official document text is typed in Courier Prime. It carries clauses, field values and anything that should feel stamped by an office.
